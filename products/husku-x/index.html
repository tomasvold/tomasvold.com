HUSKU-X is a portable, fully offline backingtrack and click-track player built for gigging musicians. It runs on a Raspberry Pi and is controlled from any phone, tablet, or laptop — no cloud, no internet connection required on stage.

Built for the stage

Everything a backingtrack rig needs

🎚️

Setlist & Playlist Management

Drag-and-drop reordering, and full setlists you can save and reload per gig, so tonight's running order is one tap away.

🥁

Built-in Click Generator

Tap tempo or dial in an exact BPM, pick a meter and subdivision, and bake a ready-to-play click track straight into your library.

🔌

Supports Mono and Stereo backingtracks

On a 2 channel audio interface the click is hard-panned right and the tracks are hard-panned left. Plug in a 4+ channel audio interface and you get a stereo backingtrack automatically routed to channel 1 and 2, with the click routed to channel 3

🎹

MIDI Sync

A matching MIDI file plays alongside your backingtrack for lighting or automation cues, with an adjustable sync offset to keep everything tight.

📶

Fully Offline

Runs entirely on a Raspberry Pi with no cloud dependency — the whole rig works even with zero internet access.

The interface

Simple enough to run from the wings

A dark, high-contrast UI designed to be read at a glance under stage lighting.

Home

Setlist & playback

Your current setlist, transport controls, and full track vault, all on one screen. Reorder tracks by dragging, upload new ones from the browser, and tap a track to load and play it instantly.

Metronome

On-the-fly click generation

Set a tempo by tapping along or using the BPM stepper, choose your meter and subdivision, and generate a five-minute click track that's automatically routed for whatever audio interface is currently plugged in.

Settings

Hardware at a glance

Switch audio interfaces on the fly, and keep an eye on storage, CPU temperature etc.
