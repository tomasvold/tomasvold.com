The easiest way to play Backingtracks!

HUSKU-X is a portable, fully offline backingtrack and click-track player built for gigging musicians. It can be controlled from any phone, tablet, or laptop — no cloud, no internet connection required on stage.

Features

🎚️

Setlist Management

Add the tracks directly from your connected device. Supports multiple setlists.

🔌

Supports Mono and Stereo backingtracks

  • On a 2 channel audio interface the click is hard-panned right and the tracks are hard-panned left (to FOH/PA)
  • Plug in a 4+ channel audio interface and you get a stereo backingtrack automatically routed to channel 1 and 2, with the click routed to channel 3
🥁

Built-in Click Generator

Tap tempo or dial in an exact BPM, pick a meter and subdivision, and create a ready-to-play click track straight into your library.

🎹

MIDI Sync

Option to play a matching MIDI file alongside your backingtrack for lighting or automation cues, with an adjustable sync offset to keep everything tight.

💾

USB Import

Plug in a USB stick with your backing tracks (MP3 or WAV) and it will automatically import to the library.

📶

Fully Offline

Runs locally on the Husku-X device with no internet connection and you don't even have to have flight mode enabled since your phone, tablet, computer is acting as the remote.

Tips for Best Usage

🎵

Creating a mono backing track

  • Pan the click track 100% right.
  • Mix your backing track down to mono, and duplicate it onto two separate tracks so it plays on both the left and right channels — your IEM mix typically only carries the right channel, so this way you'll still hear the backing track alongside the click.
  • Export as MP3 or WAV.

Watch the how-to video →

🎵

Creating a stereo backing track

  • Keep one track for the stereo backing track, and the click track on its own separate track.
  • Set your main bus to 4 channels instead of 2.
  • Route the backing track to channels 1–2 and the click track to channel 3 (a single WAV file can contain 3 channels).
  • Export as WAV.
